- **Identifier quoting**: checks names byte by byte with a class table, falling back to the rune loop for non-ASCII names; reserved-name lookup uses a first-letter/length index derived from the word list instead of a map lookup. `AST.SQL()` and formatter output are byte-identical. `BenchmarkRender` is roughly 11% to 20% faster on `sql` and 6% to 11% on `readable` on the amd64 CI runner, with unchanged allocs/op and B/op (see the PR for the measurement method and its limits).
